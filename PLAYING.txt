GET STARTED
Extract the entire Windows ZIP into a folder, then run LunarRescue.exe.
Unreal Editor is not required. Keep Engine, LunarRescue and Credits
beside the executable.

At home, press 1 or Enter for the Moon, or 2 for Mars.
Choose Apollo 12 / SELENE / ARES, then select Start mission.
At the Apollo briefing, Enter begins a new expedition.
T selects historical exploration (untimed) or a 30-minute exercise.
All game text and guidance are in English.

FIRST MINUTE
Follow the single instruction: look, walk to the gold marker, and face the kit.
Press the displayed work key to open the equipment check, then left click.
Opening a tool does not finish its action. Follow its current step to commit it.
The album shortcut appears after you have photographs or cargo.
If work input does nothing, the instruction card briefly explains how to proceed.
It updates as you approach or face the site and shows your current work key.
Left click outside a tool also requests this help. H hides it with the guide.

MY EXPEDITION AND ENDING
Press I, then choose My Expedition / map to review your recorded route.
Gold shows your walk; thin cyan shows the reconstructed historical traverse.
Site symbols link your photographs and collected cargo to their work locations.
Older checkpoints may contain only a partial route; the map explains this.
After successful stowage, a short authored cargo scene plays.
Press your displayed work key, left click or Enter to skip; Esc pauses it.

SCIENCE TOOLS
Photograph: open the camera, center the work site and left click the shutter.
ALSEP: center the level bubble, then find and verify the strongest radio signal.
Head: lift the rock with tongs, then seal its pouch.
Bench: scoop fine soil, then seal and label it.
Sharp: place the core tube, tap with the hammer four times, extract and cap it.
Surveyor: release two fittings/cable ends, then remove and pack the component.
Intrepid: open the manifest and confirm cargo stowage to finish.
Esc immediately pauses and safely cancels unfinished work. Cargo is committed only at the final action.
Let each short tool motion finish before the next input. The tool tip follows
the marked fitting; a lifted sample moves into its labeled pouch before stowing.

OPTIONAL COATING INVESTIGATION
After the whole-Surveyor photo, press K near the spacecraft. Photograph each
illustrative coating panel, then open the comparison. Q cycles explanations;
left click confirms. K returns to the main task. I retains the two photographs
and interpretation. This optional study is not required to finish the mission.
The panels illustrate NASA's returned-camera analysis; their colors and geometry
are authored and are not measured lunar reflectance or exact historical patches.

SAVING
Completed work creates an automatic checkpoint.
C continues a saved expedition from the menu, briefing or exercise ending.
Esc, then R reloads its last checkpoint.
If a photograph is still saving, wait for it to finish before reloading.
The evidence album updates automatically when a photograph finishes or fails.
A new expedition asks before replacing existing progress.
Walking after the last completed task and partial work are not saved on exit.
Keep LunarRescue/Saved when moving the game to retain saves and photographs.

HISTORY
This is an educational adaptation of Apollo 12 surface work using NASA/LROC
terrain and mapped work sites. Two historical EVAs are condensed into one
chapter. Historical exploration has no time limit. The separate 30-minute
exercise is not actual suit oxygen endurance. Tool actions are simplified.
Radio narration is original synthesized English game dialogue, not NASA audio.
Sources and asset attributions are included in Credits.

MISSION SELECTION
Explore the Moon / Explore Mars opens the mission picker.
Choose a card, then click START MISSION or press Enter.
1 / 2: choose destination; Left / Right: select mission; Esc: back; F1: settings.
Continue expedition resumes the saved Apollo checkpoint.

NEW MISSION PRESENTATION AND OPTIONAL RESEARCH
At a new-mission briefing, Enter starts the authored departure. Enter/left click skips after 0.3 seconds; Esc pauses. Mission clocks begin at surface handover.
Carried objects appear from actual pickup/committed-cargo state. Camera view (default V) changes between first and third person.
Approach and face PIP-3 or Mira's communicator and use the displayed work binding (default E), or open I > Mission Crew. They are fictional scripted guides.
Moon missions offer I > Water Research: answer by click or 1/2/3, then Enter to record after three correct decisions. Science & Sources explains remote Cabeus/2009 evidence; it does not detect water at your landing site.
Esc/F1 closes the notebook. Apollo research is retained by successful checkpoint saves; SELENE research is session-local.


MODERN FIELD TRAINING (local work, 4 November 2026)
Complete four EVA checks and open the hatch. Select and confirm a gameplay budget.
B opens resources/map/inventory. Inspect rover to unlock driving and magnifier.
W/S drives, A/D steers, your work key (default E) exits onto safe ground.
G toggles magnifier; hold left click on a labelled cartridge, release to cancel,
E packs an inspected item. Mass/storage are limited. Deposit near the lander.
Powered mission links enable timed recharge/downlink beside the resource board.
SELENE/ARES cable: isolate, choose a left port, then its matching right identifier.
Apollo ALSEP retains its historical-task flow. These are fictional modern training
procedures; Apollo 12 had no rover and cartridges never measure local water.
