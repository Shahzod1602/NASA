# Lunar water remote-study credits

This optional exercise reviews a modern LCROSS result at Cabeus. It does not add
water to the Apollo 12 terrain or change the historical sample/cargo mission.
The bundled product is a derived, offline publication-facts JSON, not an instrument
spectrum, a NASA-authored game, a new laboratory result or an extraction model.

Primary scientific source: Colaprete, A. et al. (2010), *Detection of Water in the
LCROSS Ejecta Plume*, Science 330(6003), 463-468,
[doi:10.1126/science.1186986](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1186986).
[Official PubMed abstract record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20966242/).
Scientific facts are transcribed and paraphrased; no paper, figure, copied abstract
or publisher PDF is redistributed. The derived JSON and exercise are authored by
LunarRescue contributors with AI assistance and do not supersede the source.

[LCROSS mission context](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lcross/) and
[NASA quick mission facts, Appendix C](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20130014470/downloads/20130014470.pdf)
support the mission and approximate crater context. The crater centre coordinates
are not the actual impact point and must not become a navigation target.
[NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)
apply to NASA context. Credit does not imply NASA endorsement or partnership.

The [PDS mission archive](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lcross/default.htm)
contains real raw and calibrated spectra. No spectrum is bundled or plotted here.
In particular, the published water abundance must not be inferred merely by
plotting one uncorrected archive packet. Background, geometry, calibration,
plume composition and ejecta mass analysis belong to the original research.

`Content/Science/LunarWater/manifest.json` records the factual product checksum,
size and retrieval/processing context. Dataset metadata lists the source for each
claim, units and processing steps. Record checked: 3 November 2026. The display
retains the reported plus/minus without inventing a confidence level. Its 100 kg
comparison and 2.7–8.5 percent arithmetic endpoints are explanatory calculations,
not independently measured samples or guaranteed water bounds. Public game data
contains no personal information or externally transmitted analytics.
