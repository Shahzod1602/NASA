# External model credits

Imported on 2026-09-20 from the files in D:\Users\shaxz\Downloads.
The final Unreal assets are in Content/Lunar/Imported. Meshes were converted
from GLB to static OBJ with SourceArt textures and scaled to Unreal centimeters.
The imported textures are capped at 2K for the laptop GPU.

- Apollo Lunar Module: NASA / Michael D. Carbajal.
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/apollo-lunar-module/
- Habitat Demonstration Unit, both parts: NASA / LaRC Advanced Concepts Lab.
  https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/habitat-demonstration-unit/
- Lunar Rover / Lunokhod (low poly): Akimkhan, based on work by snakemakos;
  Sketchfab CC Attribution.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lunar-rover-lunokhod-low-poly-74cec71e4bb54973a9d599ce1d4af8de
- Sci-fi Battery: andreas9343; Sketchfab CC Attribution.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sci-fi-battery-3057f7bfa48e42d2aa1bf213db6ee4e0
- Sci-fi Terminal: jellystuff; Sketchfab CC Attribution.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sci-fi-terminal-04af7eacb07541fe848bc8a258577858
- Solar Panel: rivetech; Sketchfab CC Attribution.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/solar-panel-83483a66f8974d7e8f2e7bbde518d606
- Moon Rock 01 and Moon Rock 06 diffuse textures: Poly Haven, CC0.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_01
  https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_06

The Poly Haven ZIP files contain Blender meshes. Only their diffuse textures
were used here, on existing procedural basalt meshes; the Blender geometry
was not imported. The higher-polygon Mondfahrzeug rover download was not used.
