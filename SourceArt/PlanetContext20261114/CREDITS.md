# NASA global colour illustrations — offline globe context

**Moon:** NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, *CGI Moon Kit*, ID 4720, December 2025 colour version. Visualization by Ernie Wright, with LRO/LROC/LOLA instrument-team data. Source and full credits: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

**Mars:** NASA/Jet Propulsion Laboratory & Caltech, *Mars Image Texture*, from Viking images processed at USGS. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/

The exact source JPEGs and their checksums/URLs are retained in `Raw` and `manifest.json`. No detail has been generated or presented as an additional planetary measurement. A UI sphere projection, lighting, drag rotation and zoom were implemented with OpenAI Codex assistance.

These images provide global visual context only. They do not displace terrain, supply slope measurements, determine resource costs, certify safe travel or locate fictional stations on a real planet. The Moon source explicitly states that its colour products are optimized for aesthetics, with polar filling and image processing; scientific work should use its source data. The Mars texture page does not provide a numeric CRS. Local mission positions and distances use the game's actual world coordinates on the separate local map.
