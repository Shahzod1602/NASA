# Suit feedback assets

Suit breath and step effects are original deterministic synthesized PCM waveforms, generated
with NumPy by Tools/prepare_immersion.py. They represent sound heard inside the suit.
Radio speech was synthesized locally with Windows Microsoft Zira Desktop, then filtered to
350–3300 Hz and combined with original signaling tones. It is synthetic English narration.

Glove geometry uses Unreal Engine basic sphere meshes with original material colors.
The visor rim is drawn by the HUD. Boot treads are an original procedural decal shader.
Landing debris uses a bounded pool of small mesh particles and lunar gravity.
The power switch, relay and ready cues are original synthetic PCM generated by
Tools/prepare_station_audio.py and played through the astronaut's suit audio.

The glove model is a procedural prototype. No scanned astronaut model, animation pack,
commercial sound library, or third-party recording is included.
