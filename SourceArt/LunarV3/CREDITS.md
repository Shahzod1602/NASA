# Lunar surface V3

Original deterministic procedural geometry and material graphs generated for Lunar Rescue.
No elevation survey, photogrammetry or NASA surface texture is used.

The sky continues to use the V2 star catalog and Earth imagery; see
../LunarV2/CREDITS.md and the associated license files for those sources.

Terrain: 889,778 triangles, with 50 cm sampling around the mission area and 150 cm farther out.
Crater outlines, rim heights and ejecta relief vary deterministically. The station working area
uses shallow relief to preserve navigation. Six clipped fractured rock meshes are scattered
around crater rims and across the foreground. Material color and normals use multiple noise scales.

Stars remain artistically enhanced for visibility. This scene is not a calibrated lunar exposure
or a model of a particular date or landing location.
