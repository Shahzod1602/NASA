Apollo 12 historical chapter data

NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University; LROC science team.
NASA Apollo Lunar Surface Journal.

https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/1578.pdf

Terrain: NAC_DTM_APOLLO12 (v1.9), 2 m grid spacing.
Orthophoto: NAC_DTM_APOLLO12_M120005333_2M.TIF, 2 m/pixel.
Mission mapping: APOLLO_12.ZIP, LROC spatio-temporal mapping archive.
Dataset index: https://data.lroc.im-ldi.com/lroc/view_rdr/NAC_DTM_APOLLO12
Mapping index: https://data.lroc.im-ldi.com/lroc/view_rdr/SHAPEFILE_APOLLO_12

See manifest.json for exact downloaded source URLs, SHA-256 hashes,
coordinate transformations, crop, work locations and uncertainty notes.
See APOLLO12.md for historical references and gameplay adaptations.

Small rocks, simplified Surveyor and ALSEP models, timing rules and radio
paraphrases are authored game content. Existing Apollo lander and Earth
image credits remain in the other package Credits folders.
