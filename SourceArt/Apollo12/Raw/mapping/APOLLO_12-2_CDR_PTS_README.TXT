Apollo 12 EVA 2 CDR Points
Shapefile containing the mapped locations of Commander (CDR) Pete Conrad during EVA 2 of Apollo 12.

Locations were mapped relative to the NAC_DTM_APOLLO12 orthophoto NAC_DTM_APOLLO12_M120005333_50CM.TIF, in an Equirectangular Moon projected coordinate system centered on 3°S, 180°E.
To see mapped data in context of the mission transcript, see APOLLO_12-2_DETAILED_TRANSCRIPT.CSV

When using this data, please cite:
Gonzales et al. (2020) LPS LI, Abstract #1578.  https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/1578.pdf

Attribute field descriptions:
ID: ID number assigned to CDR’s location.  ID numbers increase sequentially with mission time.
G_E_Time: Timestamp associated with CDR’s arrival at location, in ground-elapsed time (time since liftoff, reported in hours:minutes:seconds).
Status: CDR’s actions at mapped location.
