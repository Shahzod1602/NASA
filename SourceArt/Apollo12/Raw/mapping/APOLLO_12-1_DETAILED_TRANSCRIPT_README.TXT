Apollo 12 EVA 1 Detailed Transcript
CSV file of the mission dialogue for EVA 1 of Apollo 12 with associated mapping information (CDR and LMP movements, Hasselblad and ALSCC photography, and sample collection).  Dialogue is derived from the Apollo Lunar Surface Journal (https://www.nasa.gov/history/alsj/a12/a12.html), with edits to the text and timestamp rows added as appropriate.

When using this data, please cite:
Gonzales et al. (2020) LPS LI, Abstract #1578.  https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/1578.pdf

Attribute field descriptions:
GET_Hour: Hour value from the ground-elapsed time (time since takeoff).
GET_Minute:  Minute value from the ground-elapsed time (time since takeoff).
GET_Second:  Second value from the ground-elapsed time (time since takeoff).
CDR_ID: ID assigned to mapped CDR point location.
LMP_ID: ID assigned to mapped LMP point location.
Speaker: Person speaking the dialogue.  Commander, Lunar Module Pilot, Command Module Pilot, and CapCom are abbreviated as CDR, LMP, CMP, and CC, respectively.
CDR_Status: CDR’s actions associated with timestamp.
LMP_Status: LMP’s actions associated with timestamp.
Dialogue: Dialogue spoken.
Notes: Additional notes about mapping.
CDR_Stn: CDR’s current station. Station names follow the convention described in the document "Apollo 12 Voice Transcript Pertaining to the Geology of the Landing Site."
LMP_Stn: LMP’s current station. Station names follow the convention described in the document "Apollo 12 Voice Transcript Pertaining to the Geology of the Landing Site."
Hblad_Mag_Num: Hasselblad photo magazine number.
Hblad_Mag_Ltr: Hasselblad photo magazine letter.
Hblad_ID_List: IDs of Hasselblad photo(s) taken, in list format.
Hblad_Photog: Astronaut that took the Hasselblad photo(s).
Pan_name: Name of Hasselblad panorama.
Pan_descr: Description of Hasselblad panorama.
ALSCC_Mag_Num: ALSCC photo magazine number.
ALSCC_Mag_Ltr: ALSCC photo magazine letter.
ALSCC_ID_List: IDs of ALSCC photo(s) taken, in list format.
ALSCC_Photog: Astronaut that took the ALSCC photo(s).
Samp_Bag_Num: Bag that the sample was collected in.
Samp_ID_List: IDs of samples, in list format.
Samp_Type: Type of sample (core, rake, soil, etc).
