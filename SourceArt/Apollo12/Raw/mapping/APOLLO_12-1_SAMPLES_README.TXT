Apollo 12 EVA 1 Samples
Shapefile containing the mapped locations of the geology samples collected during EVA 1 of Apollo 12.  In some cases, a sample ID could not be assigned to a single location, either because samples collected over a broad area were combined into a single bag or because of insufficient information about the sample at time of collection.  In these cases, sample IDs were recorded in all locations where they may have been collected.

Note: EVA 1 samples 12002, 12003, 12005, 12009, 12011, 12012, and 12018 were unable to be located and do not appear in this dataset.

Locations were mapped relative to the NAC_DTM_APOLLO12 orthophoto NAC_DTM_APOLLO12_M120005333_50CM.TIF, in an Equirectangular Moon projected coordinate system centered on 3°S, 180°E.
To see mapped data in context of the mission transcript, see APOLLO_12-1_DETAILED_TRANSCRIPT.CSV

When using this data, please cite:
Gonzales et al. (2020) LPS LI, Abstract #1578.  https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/1578.pdf

Attribute field descriptions:
Bag: Bag in which the astronaut collected the sample(s).
ID: Sample ID number(s), in range format.  If samples were numbered out of order, sample ID numbers may fall within an ID range of a location where they were not collected.
IDList: Sample ID number(s), in list format.
IDListCont: Overflow column for listed sample IDs.
G_E_Time: Timestamp associated with sample(s), in ground-elapsed time (time since liftoff, reported in hours:minutes:seconds).
Type: Sample type (core, rake, soil, etc).
Station: Station at which the astronaut collected the sample(s).
