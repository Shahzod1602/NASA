Apollo 12 EVA 2 ALSCC Photographs
Shapefile containing the mapped acquisition points of the Apollo Lunar Surface Close-up Camera (ALSCC) photographs taken during EVA 2 of Apollo 12.  Note that ALSCC photos were only taken on EVA 2; therefore there is not a corresponding shapefile for EVA 1.

Locations were mapped relative to the NAC_DTM_APOLLO12 orthophoto NAC_DTM_APOLLO12_M120005333_50CM.TIF, in an Equirectangular Moon projected coordinate system centered on 3°S, 180°E.
To see mapped data in context of the mission transcript, see APOLLO_12-2_DETAILED_TRANSCRIPT.CSV

When using this data, please cite:
Gonzales et al. (2020) LPS LI, Abstract #1578.  https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/1578.pdf

Attribute field descriptions:
Magazine: Magazine number of the ALSCC photo(s) taken at that location.
ID: Base ID number(s) of the ALSCC photo(s) taken at that location, without the appended letter codes.
IDList: ID number(s) of the ALSCC photo(s) taken at that location, in list format.
IDListCont: Overflow column for listed ALSCC photo IDs.
G_E_Time: Timestamp associated with ALSCC photo(s), in ground-elapsed time (time since liftoff, reported in hours:minutes:seconds).
Astronaut: Astronaut that took the photo.
Station: Station at which the astronaut captured the ALSCC photo(s).
