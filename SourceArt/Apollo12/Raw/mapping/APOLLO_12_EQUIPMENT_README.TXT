Apollo 12 Equipment
Shapefile containing the mapped locations of equipment deployed during the Apollo 12 mission.

Locations were mapped relative to the NAC_DTM_APOLLO12 orthophoto NAC_DTM_APOLLO12_M120005333_50CM.TIF, in an Equirectangular Moon projected coordinate system centered on 3°S, 180°E.

When using this data, please cite:
Gonzales et al. (2020) LPS LI, Abstract #1578.  https://www.hou.usra.edu/meetings/lpsc2020/pdf/1578.pdf

Attribute field descriptions:
Equipment: Equipment name.
Station: Station at which the equipment was deployed.
